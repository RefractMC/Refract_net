Choose an instance before browsing content so Refract can filter by Minecraft version and mod loader. Search Modrinth or CurseForge from inside the app, review compatibility, and install.

Required mod dependencies are resolved automatically. Installed items can be enabled, disabled, deleted, updated, and organized into reusable mod profiles. Keep a world backup before major modpack or loader changes.
